Rooted in Tradition, Driven by Curiosity

The Story Behind Savoria

Bridging centuries-old Italian culinary heritage with modern sustainability and hospitable warmth.

From Bologna with Love

Handcrafted With Reverence For The Craft

Savoria was born out of Executive Chef Matteo Moretti’s boyhood memories rolling sfoglia pasta on marble countertops with his grandmother in Emilia-Romagna. After training in Michelin-starred kitchens across Modena and Florence, Chef Matteo set out to recreate that magic.

Every morning before sunrise, our kitchen fills with the aroma of simmering brodos and freshly kneaded semolina dough. We believe that true luxury lies in simplicity, patience, and honoring ingredients at their peak.

👨‍🍳

Chef Matteo Moretti

Executive Chef & Co-Founder

"Italian cooking is not about complicated sauces or modern gimmicks. It is about listening to the season, coaxing sweetness from a tomato, and making every guest feel like family."

Conscious Culinary Sourcing

Our Farm-to-Table Ethos

We work intimately with growers, ranchers, and fishermen who share our dedication to regenerative agriculture.

🌾

Heritage Heirloom Grains

We mill unbleached organic grains on stone disks to preserve nutritional richness and deep, nutty flavor profiles in our pastas.

🫒

Single-Estate Cold Pressed Oil

Our extra virgin olive oils are pressed exclusively from Coratina and Frantoio olives harvested on sun-drenched hills in Puglia and Tuscany.

🐟

Responsible Ocean Catch

We serve wild-caught seafood adhering strictly to Monterey Bay Aquarium Seafood Watch guidelines for marine preservation.
